Ourea Brain

Company knowledge, accessible through Slack.

What it does
Brings company context into Slack for colleagues at Ourea Energy, designed to help them retrieve project context, identify owners, and follow up on decisions.
What I built
As an Agentic AI Intern, I built and deployed the assistant: a Slack-connected app on Google Cloud Run drawing on Slack, Gmail, Google Docs, and meeting notes, using Anthropic’s Claude API.
Observed behaviour
An early live deployment answered a Slack question using meeting-note context.
